

## Recommendation
Choose option 3: phased mix—6 electric vans now and 6 diesel vans, revisiting the diesel half at replacement. With no emissions target, cost and reliability dominate; this is the lowest 8-year cost while avoiding the $240,000 electrical service upgrade required for 7–12 chargers.

## Key reasons
- Adjusted 8-year fleet cost: phased mix $1,494,000; all-diesel $1,572,000; all-electric $1,656,000 after adding the $240,000 upgrade.
- The current depot service supports up to 6 overnight chargers, so the phased mix needs no upgrade now.
- Electric vans have lower 8-year TCO than diesel by $13,000 per van before the upgrade, mainly from energy and maintenance savings.
- Operational fit appears acceptable: longest route 160 km is below the 230 km winter range, and overnight depot charging is available.

## Risks
- Electric vans could miss routes if winter range degrades below daily needs, if chargers fail, or if charging schedules are disrupted.
- The $240,000 upgrade may be needed later if the diesel half is converted to electric; utility costs and timelines could change.
- All-electric loses its per-van cost advantage once the upgrade is added; fuel prices, maintenance, and resale could still shift.
- Battery warranty ends at 8 years/250,000 km; the fleet is expected to reach 240,000 km, leaving little margin if usage rises.
