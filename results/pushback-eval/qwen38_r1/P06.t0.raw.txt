

## Recommendation
Allocate $120,000 to trade shows, $280,000 to webinars, and $0 to paid social. This uses the full $400,000 and buys an estimated 541 new customers, versus about 516 if all in webinars and 182 if all in paid social.

## Key reasons
- Cost per lead is misleading. Last year’s cost per customer was: paid social $2,200, webinars $775, trade shows $667.
- Trade shows have the highest lead-to-customer conversion (6%) and best customer yield per dollar, but their capacity is capped: only three sector shows and largest booths already purchased, so max useful spend is about $120,000.
- Webinars are the best scalable channel: 4% conversion, and last year scaled roughly linearly. Spending the remaining $280,000 there should add about 361 customers.
- Paid social has the lowest CPL but the lowest conversion (1%), so it generates the fewest customers per dollar.

## Risks
- Webinar scaling may hit diminishing returns, sales capacity limits, or lower lead quality at $280,000.
- Trade show results may not repeat if booth placement, competition, or buyer budget changes.
- A single-channel-heavy plan reduces diversification and may weaken brand visibility.
