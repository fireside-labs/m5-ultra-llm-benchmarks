

## Recommendation
Lease Mill Street. Under the given assumptions, it produces the highest expected contribution over the five-year lease and avoids Harbor Walk’s severe seasonality.

## Key reasons
- Harbor Walk’s July count is not representative. Weighting 8 months at 3,000 and 4 months at 12,000 gives an average of about 6,000 people/day.
- Annual sales margin per daily pedestrian is roughly $79: 4% × $9 × 60% × 365.
- Mill Street: 7,000/day → about $552k annual margin. Five-year rent with two months free averages about $110k/year, leaving about $442k before fixed costs.
- Harbor Walk: 6,000/day → about $473k annual margin; rent is $108k/year, leaving about $365k.
- Elm Plaza: 5,500/day → about $434k annual margin; rent is $84k/year, leaving about $350k.

After the $300k fixed cost, Mill Street is ahead by roughly $77k/year versus Harbor Walk and $92k/year versus Elm Plaza.

## Risks
The ranking depends on 4% conversion and stable Mill Street footfall. Elm Plaza’s free parking could raise conversion or ticket size; if it adds more than about 1,200 daily-equivalent pedestrians, Elm could overtake Mill. Harbor Walk has upside only if summer sales can offset winter weakness.
