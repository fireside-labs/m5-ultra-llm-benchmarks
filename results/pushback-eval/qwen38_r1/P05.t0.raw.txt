

## Recommendation
Choose Nimbus cloud backup as the firm’s backup solution. It is the only single option that satisfies the insurer’s off-site requirement, stays within the $3,000 annual budget, and meets the managing partner’s restore tolerance.

## Key reasons
Nimbus costs $18 × 10 users × 12 months = $2,160 per year, under budget. The NAS costs $5,400 upfront spread over 5 years ($1,080/year) plus $900 support, totaling $1,980/year, but it is stored in the office and therefore fails the insurer’s requirement for a copy at least 10 km away. Combining both would cost about $4,140/year, exceeding the budget. Nimbus stores copies in two data centres more than 300 km away and provides immutable 90-day version history, giving stronger ransomware protection than NAS snapshots that an admin can delete. A 1–2 day full restore is acceptable, and individual files can be restored in minutes.

## Risks
If Nimbus is ever unavailable, full recovery could take up to 2 days by download or 1 day by courier. The firm should test restores and maintain an updated client contact list for outage response.
