

## Recommendation
Choose Orrin Fabrication for all 8,000 frames per year for the 3-year contract, with contractual quality and delivery safeguards.

## Key reasons
Expected annual cost favors Orrin: $352,000 purchase + $7,200 rework = $359,200. Kestrel all costs $328,000 + $43,200 = $371,200. Dual-sourcing costs $340,000 + $25,200 rework + $30,000 admin = $395,200. Orrin’s 1% defect rate more than offsets its $3 higher unit price, saving about $12,000/year versus Kestrel and $36,000/year versus dual-sourcing. Orrin’s 12,000 annual capacity covers the 8,000-unit requirement, and lead time/payment terms are identical. Fewer defects also mean fewer 2-day bike delays. Purchasing’s price-only scorecard should be replaced by landed cost including rework and service risk.

## Risks
Orrin is a single-source dependency, and capacity headroom is 4,000 frames/year, less than Kestrel’s. The 1% defect estimate comes from a small ~1,000-frame trial, so true defect rates may differ. If Orrin suffers disruption or quality deterioration, fallback options are limited and Kestrel carries higher defect risk. Mitigate with audits, performance clauses, safety stock, and a qualified backup plan.
