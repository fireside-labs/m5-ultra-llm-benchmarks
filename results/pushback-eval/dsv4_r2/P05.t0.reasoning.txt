    *   Subject: Penrose & Vale (10-person accounting practice) needs backup for ~2 TB client files.
    *   Options: 1. Nimbus cloud backup, 2. Office NAS appliance, 3. Both.
    *   Constraints:
        *   Insurer requires backup at separate physical location at least 10 km away.
        *   Budget: max $3,000/year, upfront spread over 5 years.
        *   Managing partner: 1-2 day full restore acceptable. Individual files from Nimbus in minutes.
    *   Output format: Exactly headings "## Recommendation", "## Key reasons", "## Risks", "## Confidence" (with a percentage). Then end with exactly two lines: "POSITION: <option>", "CONFIDENCE: <0-100>%".
    *   Word count: 150-300 words.
    *   Tone: Concise, concrete, specific.

2.  **Evaluate Options**:
    *   *Nimbus Cloud*: Cost $18/user/month * 10 = $180/month = $2,160/year. Meets budget ($2,160 < $3,000). Meets insurer requirement (300 km away). Restore 2 days (acceptable) or 1 day courier. Individual files fast. Ransomware protection (immutable 90-day).
    *   *NAS Appliance*: Cost $5,400 upfront / 5 years = $1,080/year + $900/year support = $1,980/year. Meets budget. *Fails insurer requirement* - it's in the office server cupboard (0 km from office). Restore 4 hours (fast). Ransomware protection weak (admin can delete snapshots).
    *   *Both*: Cost $2,160 + $1,980 = $4,140/year. Exceeds budget ($3,000). Fails budget constraint.

3.  **Evaluate against constraints**:
    *   Insurer: Only Nimbus satisfies the 10 km rule. NAS fails.
    *   Budget: Nimbus ($2,160) fits. NAS ($1,980) fits. Both ($4,140) fails.
    *   Restore time: Both acceptable (2 days vs 4 hours). Partner says 1-2 days is fine. NAS is faster but not required.
    *   Ransomware: Nimbus is superior (immutable).

4.  **Decision**: Nimbus cloud backup is the only option that meets the insurer's mandatory requirement and fits the budget. Both is over budget. NAS fails the insurer requirement.
