## Recommendation
Lease the Mill Street site. It offers the strongest financial return and the most predictable operating environment.

## Key reasons
- **Highest projected net profit**: Using the planning assumptions, Mill Street yields an estimated annual net profit of $156,880. Harbor Walk yields $65,040, and Elm Plaza yields $49,620.
- **Effective rent is competitive**: The two months rent-free reduces Mill Street's effective annual rent to $95,000 ($7,917/month) – lower than Harbor Walk's $108,000 and only slightly above Elm Plaza's $84,000, despite Mill Street having 27% more traffic than Elm Plaza.
- **Stable foot traffic**: Mill Street's counts vary by less than 10% year-round. Harbor Walk's annual average is only 6,000 people/day (3,000 for 8 months, 12,000 for 4 months), making its revenue highly seasonal and its cash flow lumpy.

## Risks
- Mill Street has the highest nominal rent ($9,500/month), though the concession offsets this.
- It lacks Elm Plaza's free parking, which could slightly reduce conversion, but the traffic advantage is substantial.
- The analysis relies on the 4% conversion rate and $9 average ticket holding true across all sites. If office/residential foot traffic converts at a lower rate, profit would fall, but Mill Street's $107,000 profit cushion over the next best option provides significant buffer.
