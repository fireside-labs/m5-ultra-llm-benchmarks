- **Insurer compliance**: The insurer mandates a copy at least 10 km away. Nimbus stores data in data centres 300+ km away. The NAS sits in the office cupboard (0 km), failing this mandatory condition and risking loss of professional-liability cover.
- **Budget fit**: Nimbus costs $2,160/year ($18 x 10 x 12), within the $3,000 cap. The NAS costs $1,980/year ($5,400/5 + $900), but is non-compliant. "Both" costs $4,140/year, exceeding the cap.

## Risks
- Full restore is slower than a local NAS (2 days vs 4 hours), but this is an accepted trade-off.
- Dependency on internet bandwidth for backup; the courier-drive option mitigates large restore risks.
- Cloud provider lock-in or service failure, though dual-region redundancy reduces this risk.
