## Recommendation
Lease Mill Street. On seasonally adjusted numbers it is the most profitable site by a wide margin — roughly **$142k/year** profit, versus ~$66k at Harbor Walk and ~$50k at Elm Plaza.

## Key reasons
- **Harbor Walk's 12,000/day count is a July figure.** Blending June–Sept at 12,000/day with Oct–May at 3,000/day gives ~6,000/day average. That yields revenue ≈ 6,000 × 365 × 4% × $9 ≈ $789k/year; after 60% margin ($474k), rent ($108k) and fixed costs ($300k), profit ≈ **$66k**.
- **Mill Street:** 7,000/day year-round → revenue ≈ $920k, margin $552k, rent $114k (effectively ~$110k/yr averaged over the lease with two months free), fixed $300k → profit ≈ **$142k** (year 1 ≈ $157k).
- **Elm Plaza:** 5,500/day → revenue ≈ $723k, margin $434k, rent $84k, fixed $300k → profit ≈ **$50k**.
- Mill Street's count is stable year-round (±10% per the survey firm), so the estimate is reliable; Harbor Walk's is not.

## Risks
- The 4% conversion and $9 ticket come from existing shops; tourists at Harbor Walk or office workers at Mill Street may behave differently. Harbor Walk only wins if its conversion is ~2.5× Mill Street's.
- Elm Plaza's free parking could lift conversion above 4% — no data to quantify this.
- Counts come from a single week in July; an off-season recount at all sites would firm up the numbers.
