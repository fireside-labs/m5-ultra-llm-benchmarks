## Recommendation
Select Nimbus cloud backup only.

## Key reasons
- It is the only option that satisfies the insurer's mandatory condition for a backup copy stored at a separate physical location at least 10 km away (Nimbus is 300+ km away; the NAS is in the office cupboard).
- It fits the budget: $2,160 per year ($18 × 10 users × 12 months) versus the $3,000 cap. The NAS costs $1,980 per year when amortized, but fails the insurer test, and "both" would cost $4,140/year, exceeding the cap.
- The full restore time (2 days by download, 1 day by courier) is within the managing partner's stated 1–2 day acceptable range.
- Nimbus offers immutable 90-day version history, which is stronger ransomware protection than the NAS's admin-deletable snapshots.
- Individual file restores in minutes is a practical advantage for daily client work.

## Risks
- Full restore depends on internet speed; if bandwidth is poor, the 2-day estimate could stretch. Mitigate by pre-arranging the courier-drive option for disasters.
- Recurring subscription cost is slightly higher than the amortized NAS, but still well under budget.
- Reliance on a third-party provider, though the two-region redundancy reduces this risk.
