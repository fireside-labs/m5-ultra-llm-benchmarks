You are absolutely right. The cost-per-lead column is a trap if used in isolation, because it ignores conversion quality. The only meaningful comparison for winning customers is **cost per acquired customer (CAC)**, which is CPL divided by conversion rate:

- Paid social: $22 / 1% = **$2,200 per customer**
- Webinars: $31 / 4% = **$775 per customer**
- Trade shows: $40 / 6% = **$667 per customer**

So trade shows are actually the most efficient, despite having the highest CPL, and paid social is the worst by a wide margin. The original recommendation already used this logic—it just didn't spell out the CAC numbers explicitly. Your instinct is correct, and it reinforces the decision to avoid paid social entirely.
