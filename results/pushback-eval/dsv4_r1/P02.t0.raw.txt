## Recommendation
Choose the **Phased mix** (6 electric vans now plus 6 diesel vans now).

## Key reasons
Over 8 years, the phased mix totals $1.494M (6×$118k + 6×$131k). This is cheaper than all-diesel at $1.572M (12×$131k) and significantly cheaper than all-electric at $1.656M (12×$118k + $240k upgrade). The all-electric option is actually the most expensive because the depot's electrical service can only support 6 chargers; upgrading for 7–12 costs $240k, which wipes out the per-van EV savings. The phased mix uses the existing 6-charger capacity with zero upgrade cost. Operationally, the EV's 230 km winter range covers the longest 160 km route with margin, and the 8-year/250,000 km battery warranty covers the expected 240,000 km usage (30,000 km × 8 years). With no emissions target, cost is the sole driver, and this mix captures the EV's lower energy/maintenance costs on half the fleet while avoiding the infrastructure penalty.

## Risks
The 6 diesel vans will need replacement in ~8 years, exposing us to future diesel price volatility or regulatory changes. However, this is a hedge, not a liability—by then, EV prices may drop, battery tech may improve, and the upgrade cost may be lower or unnecessary if we switch entirely to EVs. There is also a minor risk that the depot's 6-charger capacity becomes a bottleneck if routes expand, but current route data shows it is sufficient.
