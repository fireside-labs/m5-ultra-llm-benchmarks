

## Recommendation
Allocate $120,000 to trade shows and $280,000 to webinars; put $0 in paid social. This is a split across channels, not all into paid social.

## Key reasons
- Cost per lead is misleading. Expected first-year customers: paid social 50 from $110k = $2,200/customer; webinars 120 from $93k = $775/customer; trade shows 180 from $120k = $667/customer.
- Trade shows are the most efficient channel, but capacity appears fixed: only three sector shows, already largest booth package, and no other shows. So assume $120k is the practical trade-show ceiling.
- Webinars are the best scalable channel: last year incremental budget scaled roughly linearly. At that rate, $280k would produce about 361 expected customers, versus about 127 from the same $280k in paid social.
- All into paid social buys the most leads but fewest customers.

## Risks
- Webinar performance may not scale linearly at 3x last year's spend; monitor weekly cost per customer.
- Trade-show conversion may depend on seasonality, booth location, or sales follow-up.
- Concentrating in webinars increases dependency on one channel.
