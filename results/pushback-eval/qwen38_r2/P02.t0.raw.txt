

## Recommendation
Choose the phased mix: 6 electric vans now plus 6 diesel vans, revisiting the diesel half when those vans are replaced. It avoids the $240,000 electrical upgrade and has the lowest known 8-year fleet TCO: $1,494,000, versus $1,572,000 for all-diesel and $1,656,000 for all-electric including the upgrade.

## Key reasons
- Only 6 chargers are needed, within existing service.
- Electric vans meet duty cycle: 30,000 km/year totals 240,000 km, within the 250,000 km battery warranty; 230 km winter range exceeds the 160 km longest route, with overnight charging.
- Electric TCO is lower per van, but the 12-van upgrade erases most savings; phased mix captures partial savings while preserving flexibility.

## Risks
- If diesel vans are replaced sooner, future electric/charging costs are unknown; assume no upgrade needed for current 6 chargers.
- Winter range margin is 70 km; detours, heating, or charging failures could affect reliability.
- Mixed fleet adds maintenance and training complexity.
