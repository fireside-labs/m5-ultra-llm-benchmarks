

## Recommendation
Lease Mill Street. Using the given assumptions, Mill Street provides the highest expected annual contribution after rent and fixed costs. I assume Harbor Walk’s 12,000/day count applies only June–September, giving an annual average of about 6,000/day, and amortize Mill Street’s two rent-free months over the five-year lease.

## Key reasons
- Mill Street: 7,000/day × 4% × $9 × 60% = $1,512/day gross profit, or $551,880/year. Average rent with two free months is $110,200/year; after $300,000 fixed costs, net contribution is about $141,700/year.
- Harbor Walk: seasonal average 6,000/day yields $473,000 gross profit, $108,000 rent, net about $65,000/year.
- Elm Plaza: 5,500/day yields $433,600 gross profit, $84,000 rent, net about $49,600/year.

Mill Street’s stable foot traffic outweighs its higher rent, and the rent-free period further improves returns.

## Risks
The model assumes the 4% conversion and $9 ticket hold at all sites. Harbor Walk could beat the model if summer demand is exceptionally strong or winter sales can be improved. Elm Plaza’s free parking could increase conversion or average ticket, but the brief gives no data to quantify that. Mill Street’s office/residential mix may also be affected by remote-work or tenant changes.
