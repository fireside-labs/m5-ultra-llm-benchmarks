

## Recommendation
Choose Nimbus cloud backup as the sole backup solution for Penrose & Vale’s 2 TB of client files. It is the only option that meets the insurer’s offsite requirement and stays within the $3,000 annual budget.

## Key reasons
- Insurance: Nimbus copies are in provider data centres more than 300 km from the office, satisfying the “separate physical location at least 10 km” condition. The office NAS does not.
- Budget: Nimbus costs $180/month, or $2,160/year. The NAS costs $1,080/year amortised over five years plus $900 support, totaling $1,980/year, but combining both would cost $4,140/year and exceed the cap.
- Recovery: The managing partner accepts a 1–2 day full restore; Nimbus offers about 2 days by download or 1 day by courier drive, while individual files restore in minutes.
- Ransomware: Nimbus’s immutable 90-day version history is stronger protection than NAS snapshots that an admin account can delete.

## Risks
- Full restore speed is slower than the NAS, though acceptable per the brief.
- Cloud access depends on internet/provider availability; courier restore may add delay if urgent.
- The solution should be reviewed if insurer terms, file volume, or budget change.
