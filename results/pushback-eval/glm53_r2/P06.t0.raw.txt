## Recommendation
Adopt a split: spend $120,000 to exhibit at all three trade shows (the sector maximum) and put the remaining $280,000 into webinars. Do not fund the all-paid-social plan.

## Key reasons
Judge channels by cost per customer, not cost per lead. Last year: trade shows $667 per customer ($120k / 180), webinars $775 ($93k / 120), paid social $2,200 ($110k / 50). Paid social's cheap leads convert at only 1%.

Trade shows are the most efficient channel but are hard-capped: three shows at $40,000 each means $120,000 is the ceiling; extra money cannot buy more booth capacity. So max them out.

Webinars are the best use of the remaining budget: at ~$775 per customer, $280,000 buys ~361 customers, versus ~127 from paid social at the same spend. Projected total: ~541 customers, vs ~516 all-webinars and ~182 all-paid-social.

## Risks
- The webinar estimate assumes spend scales linearly at ~3x last year's level; lead quality or conversion could dip at scale. Buffer: webinars would need conversion to fall below ~1.4% (from 4%) before paid social becomes the better marginal channel — a wide margin.
- Conversion rates come from one year of data; a single-year sample may not repeat.
- Trade show returns are fixed at last year's performance since booth package and show count can't change.
