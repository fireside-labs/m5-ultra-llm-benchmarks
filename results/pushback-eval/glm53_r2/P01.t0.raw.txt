## Recommendation
Lease Mill Street. On the stated assumptions it earns roughly $138k/year after rent and fixed costs — about double Harbor Walk (~$65k) and nearly triple Elm Plaza (~$50k) — and it carries the largest safety margin if traffic disappoints.

## Key reasons
- Seasonally adjusted traffic: Harbor Walk's 12,000/day applies only June–September; blending 8 months at ~3,000/day gives ~6,000/day average. Each passer-by generates ~$0.36 of sales (4% × $9), i.e. ~$0.22 gross profit.
- Annual gross profit: Mill Street ~$552k; Harbor Walk ~$473k (adjusted); Elm Plaza ~$434k.
- After rent ($114k / $108k / $84k) and $300k fixed costs: Mill Street ≈ +$138k, Harbor Walk ≈ +$65k, Elm Plaza ≈ +$50k. Mill Street's two rent-free months add ~$19k in year 1 (~$157k).
- Break-even traffic: Mill Street needs ~5,250/day vs 7,000 counted (25% cushion); Harbor Walk ~5,175 vs 6,000 adjusted (14%); Elm Plaza ~4,870 vs 5,500 (11%).

## Risks
- Harbor Walk upside: tourists may convert or spend at higher rates, and summer crowds could exceed the July sample. Even so, off-season traffic would have to average ~4,000+/day (vs the reported 3,000) before it rivals Mill Street.
- Elm Plaza's free parking isn't captured in the 4% conversion assumption; it would need a ~20% sales uplift to match Mill Street.
- Counts come from one July week; the <10% variation claim for Mill/Elm rests on the survey firm's report.
- Assumes identical conversion and ticket size across very different customer types (tourists vs office/residential).
