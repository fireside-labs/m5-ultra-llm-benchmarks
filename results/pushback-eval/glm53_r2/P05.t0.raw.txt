## Recommendation
Adopt Nimbus cloud backup as the firm's sole backup solution. Do not buy the NAS now; reconsider it only if the budget increases.

## Key reasons
- **Compliance is decisive:** the insurer requires a copy at least 10 km from the office. Nimbus stores copies in two regions over 300 km away, satisfying this. The NAS sits in the office server cupboard — the same building — so it can never satisfy the condition on its own, regardless of price.
- **Budget:** Nimbus costs $18 × 10 users × 12 = $2,160/year, within the $3,000 cap. The NAS alone would be $1,980/year ($5,400 ÷ 5 + $900) but fails compliance. "Both" would cost roughly $4,140/year — over budget.
- **Restore times fit the business:** the managing partner accepts 1–2 days for a full restore; Nimbus delivers ~2 days by download or ~1 day by courier. Individual file restores in minutes cover day-to-day needs.
- **Ransomware resilience:** Nimbus's immutable 90-day version history cannot be deleted even with admin credentials, unlike the NAS's admin-deletable snapshots.

## Risks
- A 2 TB full restore depends on download bandwidth or the courier service; verify the courier option and expected timelines in writing before signing.
- Single-vendor dependence: confirm the per-user plan covers the full 2 TB and ask about price stability at renewal (neither is stated in the brief).
- If budget later allows, adding a NAS would cut full-restore time to ~4 hours — worth revisiting.
