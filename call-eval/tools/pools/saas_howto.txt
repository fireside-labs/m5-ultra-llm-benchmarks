// Customer asks unrelated how-to questions while waiting (logs loading, pages slow). Neutral product talk.
C: While that loads, can I ask a random question? How do I archive old projects? We have like three hundred and I can't find anything.
A: Sure. Open the project, click the three dots in the top right, and choose "Archive." Archived projects disappear from the sidebar but they're still searchable.
C: Oh, that's easy. I'll do a cleanup day.
A: There's also a bulk option in Admin, Projects, if you want to do a bunch at once.
C: Even better.
===
C: Another random one. Can we add people from outside the company? Like contractors?
A: Yes, as guests. Guests only see the projects you invite them to. They don't count as full seats on your plan.
C: Oh, nice, I didn't know that.
A: It's under Members, "Invite guest."
===
C: Is there a dark mode? Half the office has been asking. [laughs]
A: [laughs] Yes, under your profile, Preferences, Appearance. It's per user.
C: I'm going to send that to everyone. They'll love me for five minutes.
===
C: Can we make project templates? Like, every time we do a new store install it's the same forty tasks.
A: Yes. Set up one project the way you like it, then three dots, "Save as template." Then new projects can start from it.
C: Oh, that would save so much time.
A: People love templates once they find them.
===
C: Does the mobile app do notifications? Some of the field guys say they never get anything.
A: It does, but each person has to allow notifications on their phone the first time the app asks. If they tapped "Don't allow," they have to turn it on in their phone settings.
C: That's definitely what happened. They tap "no" on everything.
A: [laughs] Very common.
===
C: How long do you keep deleted stuff? Someone deleted a task list last month and freaked out.
A: Deleted items stay in the trash for thirty days, and workspace owners can restore them. After that they're gone.
C: Thirty days. Okay. Good to know for next time.
===
C: Can I export everything? Like, if we ever needed a backup?
A: Yes, owners can request a full workspace export under Admin, Data. It's a zip file, and it can take a few hours for a big workspace.
C: Okay. I'm going to do that once a quarter, now that I think about it.
A: That's a good habit.
===
C: Is there a way to see who's using it and who isn't? Like, who never logs in?
A: The Members page has a "Last active" column, and you can sort by it. Owners can also download a usage report.
C: Oh, perfect. I suspect some people haven't logged in since we bought it. [laughs]
A: [laughs] There's always a few.
===
C: Can tasks repeat? Like, every Monday, check the fire extinguishers?
A: Yes, open the task, click the due date, and set "Repeat." Weekly, monthly, whatever you need.
C: Oh, great. Facilities will love that.
===
C: Can I change the colors of the project labels? Ours are all gray and nobody can tell them apart.
A: Yes, Settings, Labels. Click any label to pick a color.
C: Color-coding. My favorite thing. [laughs]
===
C: What happens to someone's tasks when they leave the company?
A: When you deactivate them, their tasks stay, but they become unassigned, and the project owners get a notification so they can reassign them.
C: Okay, that's good. We had someone leave and nobody knew what she was working on.
===
C: Is there a keyboard shortcut for making a new task? I'm a keyboard person.
A: "N" anywhere in a project, and question mark shows you all the shortcuts.
C: Question mark. Oh, there's a whole list. I'm going to be insufferable now. [laughs]
A: [laughs] Power user.
===
C: Can we connect it to our chat app? So people get updates there?
A: Yes, there's an integration under Admin, Integrations. You pick which projects post to which channels.
C: Okay. Putting that on the list. The list is getting long.
===
C: Is there a limit on file attachments? Someone tried to upload a huge video.
A: On your plan it's two gigabytes per file. Videos are usually better as a link.
C: Okay. I'll tell him. He uploads everything. [laughs]
===
C: Can people comment on tasks by email? Some of our older staff won't log in.
A: Yes, they can reply to the notification email and it shows up as a comment on the task.
C: Oh, that's going to be a game-changer for some of them.
===
C: Does it do time zones properly? We've got the two offices.
A: Yes, everyone sees due dates in their own time zone, based on their profile setting.
C: Okay. That's been a source of fights before. [laughs]
A: [laughs] Time zones cause a lot of fights.
===
C: Can I make some projects private? Like for HR stuff?
A: Yes. When you create the project, set it to "Private," and only people you add can see it, not even other admins unless they're added.
C: Good. HR will want that.
===
C: Is there an API? Our developer asked me last week.
A: Yes, there's a public API. The documentation is linked at the bottom of the admin console. API tokens are created under Admin, Integrations.
