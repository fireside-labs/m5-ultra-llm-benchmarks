## Case for

1. Safety: removing cars sharply cuts pedestrian injuries and deaths.
2. Cleaner air and less noise in the core.
3. Pedestrianized streets increase foot traffic and dwell time, which boosts spending.
4. Street and parking space can be reallocated to people, plazas and green space.
5. Climate: cuts carbon emissions from short car trips.
6. Buses get faster and more reliable once cars are out of the core.
