## Missed issues

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Patients readmitted at other hospitals may be missed.
4. Same patient could appear multiple times - duplicates inflate counts.
5. Age differences between groups could confound the result.
6. Stratified by tier, the program looks worse (5.0% vs 4.0%; 25% vs 22%); the headline gap is driven by case mix.
7. The improvement may be due to another intervention (bedside medication delivery) rather than the calls.
8. The readmission definition changed on July 1, which mechanically lowers program rates.

## Top 3 priorities

1. Patients readmitted at other hospitals may be missed.
2. Small samples and no uncertainty: the Spanish-language '100% reduction' is 2/10 vs 0/9, and no confidence intervals are given anywhere
3. The forecast double-counts the effect: a 50% reduction is assumed although the within-tier data show no benefit, and it is applied to all flagged patients
