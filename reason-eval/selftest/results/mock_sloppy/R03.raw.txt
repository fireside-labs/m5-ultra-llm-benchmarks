## Missed issues

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Patients with urgent needs might be turned away for an unpaid balance.
4. No hardship waiver or grace for a first offense.
5. Patients won't know about the new fee before it starts.
6. Charging Medicaid members the no-show fee would breach our payer contracts.
7. Balance holds create a barrier to access; patients may delay chronic-care visits and end up in the ED.
8. If both double-booked patients show, everyone waits longer and providers burn out.

## Top 3 priorities

1. Patients with urgent needs might be turned away for an unpaid balance.
2. Two commercial contracts require 90 days' written notice to the plan before a new patient-facing fee; the memo's 60-day patient notice doesn't cover it
3. The fee doesn't address the actual causes of no-shows (transportation 38%, work/childcare 19%); fixes for those are missing
