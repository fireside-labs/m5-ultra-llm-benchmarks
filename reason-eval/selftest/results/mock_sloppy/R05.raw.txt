## Variables

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Communication with the team could be improved.
4. There may be risks that need further analysis.
5. Projected visit volume based on demographics and disease burden.
6. Competitor urgent care across the street and existing capacity nearby.
7. Build-out cost including X-ray shielding and time to open.
8. Visibility and signage for a walk-in urgent care.
9. Partnerships with employers, schools and community organizations.

## Top 3 priorities

1. Regulatory and zoning approvals: zoning/permitted use, permitting, licensing and timelines for medical/imaging use
2. Total real-estate and build-out cost: rent, lease terms, renovation, imaging shielding and time to open
3. Referral and downstream flows: links to the hospital, specialists, imaging and lab; keeping patients in-network
