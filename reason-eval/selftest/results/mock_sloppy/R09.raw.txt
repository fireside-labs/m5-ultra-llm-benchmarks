## First steps

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Pull call volume by 30-minute interval and stagger agent schedules to match the arrival pattern.
4. Fix EHR workflows and training to bring handle time back down.
5. Measure the wait distribution (90th percentile), not the mean.
6. Portal-based fixes miss the 65% who aren't enrolled, often older patients.

## Top 3 priorities

1. The 9-minute figure excludes the 21% who hang up: measure abandonment and true demand, not just answered-call wait
2. Menu and routing: 7 IVR options and 26% transfers waste capacity; simplify routing
3. Define the target properly: service level by interval (e.g., % answered within X seconds) and abandonment, not an average
