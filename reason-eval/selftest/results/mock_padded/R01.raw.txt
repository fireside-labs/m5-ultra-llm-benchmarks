## Missed issues

1. Security / compliance certification of the vendors was not checked
2. Integration with the ERP was not verified
3. Vendor support levels / hours were not compared
4. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
5. The timeline seems aggressive.
6. Communication with the team could be improved.
7. There may be risks that need further analysis.
8. Price criterion uses a discounted year-1 price; renewal list price $303k with up to 8% increases plus a $95k implementation fee make Tallis the most expensive over 3 years
9. Tallis cannot handle the union shift-differential and overtime-averaging rules (only 'on the roadmap'), while the other two configured them in the sandbox
10. Tallis references are small, single-site, non-union customers; nothing shows it works at 1,400 employees across 9 sites with unions
11. Features were scored by one person from vendor-scripted demos; the payroll team that will use the system never saw them
12. Uptime figures are marketing claims; Tallis offers no uptime SLA while the others contract 99.9% with credits
13. Lock-in: proprietary data export and a 6-month termination fee make leaving Tallis expensive
14. Vendor viability: a 3-year-old 40-person startup mid-fundraise is a risk for mission-critical payroll
15. Scoring arithmetic: the weights add up to 110%, not 100%
16. Artificial urgency: the expiring year-1 price pressures a rushed decision on a system needed in 11 months

## Top 3 priorities

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Communication with the team could be improved.
