## Missed issues

1. Networking between two servers for distributed training was not considered
2. Warranty / hardware support not considered
3. The memo doesn't compare buying with renting cloud capacity
4. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
5. The timeline seems aggressive.
6. Communication with the team could be improved.
7. There may be risks that need further analysis.
8. Single point of failure: one box runs both training and the paying customer's live endpoint; any failure or maintenance takes everything down (with next-business-day repair)
9. Utilization mismatch: 85% of jobs use 1-2 GPUs and only 3% use 8, so the interconnect advantage rarely matters while two boxes reduce queueing between users
10. Lead time: the 8-GPU server takes 16 weeks to arrive, but the paying pilot starts in 6 weeks (4-GPU servers ship in 3)
11. Power: the 8-GPU server draws ~10.2 kW but the rack has only 6.5 kW free (8 kW limit minus 1.5 kW), so it needs a second rack too
12. The Harrow contract requires dedicated hardware not shared with any other workload; one shared box for training and Harrow violates it, whereas one of two servers can be dedicated
13. Rent the rare 8-GPU fine-tune in the cloud (~30 h x $98 = ~$3k per quarter) instead of buying for it
14. Resale value: 4-GPU servers keep ~55% after 2 years vs ~40% for 8-GPU systems with fewer buyers
15. Capital flexibility: with 14 months of runway and a board request to keep capex flexible, buying one 4-GPU server now and a second later is possible only with Option B

## Top 3 priorities

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Communication with the team could be improved.
