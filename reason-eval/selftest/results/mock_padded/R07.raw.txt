## Case for

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Communication with the team could be improved.
4. There may be risks that need further analysis.
5. Safety: far fewer traffic deaths and injuries, especially for pedestrians and cyclists
6. Public health: cleaner air and less noise in the densest part of the city
7. Economic vitality: more foot traffic, dwell time and spending; space for outdoor dining and events
8. Space efficiency: road and parking space is reallocated to people, transit lanes, green space or housing
9. Climate: lower emissions and a nudge toward low-carbon travel
10. Transit and mode shift: buses run faster and more reliably without car congestion, making transit and cycling more attractive
11. Business harm: shops and restaurants lose customers who drive, especially suburban shoppers, and may relocate to malls
12. Accessibility: disabled and elderly people and others with limited mobility depend on door-to-door car access
13. Equity: people from areas with poor transit, shift workers and lower-income commuters lose access to downtown jobs and services
14. Displacement: traffic and parking pressure move to the surrounding neighborhoods and ring roads
15. Logistics and services: deliveries, trades, emergency vehicles and residents' needs are harder to serve
16. Transit readiness and implementation: without enough transit capacity first, access collapses; enforcement costs, political backlash
17. Are the alternatives (transit, cycling, park-and-ride, accessible options) good enough that people who drive now can still reach downtown?
18. What is the net effect on downtown businesses and the local economy in this specific city (more walkers vs fewer drivers)?
19. Does car traffic disappear (mode shift / fewer trips) or just move to surrounding areas?
20. Values: how to weigh residents' and pedestrians' quality of life against access for people who drive in, and who bears the costs

## Top 3 priorities

1. Stakeholders should be consulted more broadly.
2. The timeline seems aggressive.
3. Communication with the team could be improved.
