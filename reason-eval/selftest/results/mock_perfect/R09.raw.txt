## First steps

1. Staff to the arrival pattern: Monday 8-11 gets 22% of calls while everyone works an identical 8-5 shift; measure demand by interval and stagger/shift schedules
2. Remove refill-status calls (31%) at the source: proactive text/portal status updates and pharmacy notifications
3. Failure demand: 18% call 2+ times about the same issue; find why first contact doesn't resolve it
4. Handle time rose from 4.1 to 5.6 min after the EHR switch (~37% more workload); fix EHR workflow/training
5. The 9-minute figure excludes the 21% who hang up: measure abandonment and true demand, not just answered-call wait
6. Menu and routing: 7 IVR options and 26% transfers waste capacity; simplify routing
7. Define the target properly: service level by interval (e.g., % answered within X seconds) and abandonment, not an average
8. Fill the 2 vacancies first and understand attrition before adding 4 new positions
9. Callback/virtual hold improves experience and abandonment but adds no capacity; work is deferred, not removed
10. Portal deflection only reaches the 35% with active portals; activation and other channels matter (equity, older patients)
