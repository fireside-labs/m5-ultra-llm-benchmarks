## Estimate chain

1. EV count: households x vehicles per household x EV share (8-16%) gives ~33k-67k EVs
2. Home-charging access: 25-35% of EV owners lack home charging and depend on public fast charging
3. Session frequency: 200 mi/week x 70% from DC fast / 150 mi per session = ~0.9 sessions/week for drivers without home charging
4. Drivers with home charging still add substantial load: ~2 sessions/month x the majority of EVs (~16k sessions/week, comparable to the no-home group)
5. Ride-hail and delivery fleets: 1,500 EVs x 5 sessions/week = 7,500 sessions/week
6. Port throughput: 25% of 24 h = 6 h = 360 min / 40 min = ~9 sessions per port per day
7. Subtract the 46 existing ports
8. Budget arithmetic: $12M / $140k-$220k = ~55-86 ports, a small fraction of the need
9. Leverage other sources: private charging operators, utilities, grants, and cheaper Level 2 / workplace charging
10. Phase and target: put first ports where residents lack home charging; build in stages and revisit as adoption becomes clearer
